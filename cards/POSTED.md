## Why the fixed price post is not on LinkedIn

It argues for the company's own commercial model, and the LinkedIn account is
topically about automation surviving production. Search finds a post like that
when a buyer already has the question; a feed interrupts people who did not ask,
and a vendor arguing for its own pricing reads as a pitch rather than a insight.

Its job is sales enablement. Send it to a specific prospect when the pricing
conversation comes up, instead of explaining the same thing on a call.

It suits X better, where engagement model debates are native, but that audience is
mostly peers rather than buyers, so expect discussion rather than leads.

## Before you post

- The card system and its copy rules are in `README.md`, the site copy rules in
  `../SPEC.md`. Both bind post text as much as page text.
- LinkedIn renders no markdown. Blocks of two to three sentences, blank line
  between, hook in the first two lines before the "see more" cutoff.
- Attaching an image means there is no link card underneath, so write "link in the
  comments" rather than "link below" and post the URL as the first comment.
- X takes the short version or a thread, never the LinkedIn length.

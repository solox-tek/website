Before you automate anything

Three tests
that do most of the work.

  1. Does it happen often?

    Automation pays back per execution. Twenty minutes, forty times a month.

  2. Are the rules known?

    If two people would do it the same way, it can be encoded. If not, it is a job.

  3. Is a mistake visible and cheap?

    Pick the one caught in review, not the one that reaches a customer.

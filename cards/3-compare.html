Fixed price vs time and materials

Neither is cheaper.
They just move the risk.

Fixed price

The vendor absorbs the cost when reality differs from the plan.

So the quote carries a risk premium.

Time and materials

You absorb it. You pay for hours as they happen.

So there is no ceiling.

So the question is not which model is better. It is how much is genuinely unknown, and who is better placed to carry it.
